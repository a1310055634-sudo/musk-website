他谈做生意

五句话，反复出现在他的交易里。

注：以上五句为广泛征引的公开表述，精确年份与场合待考——本站不杜撰出处。

“When something is important enough, you do it even if the odds are not in your favor.”

当一件事足够重要，即使胜算不在你这边，你也要去做。

“If things are not failing, you are not innovating enough.”

没有经历失败，说明创新得还不够。

“I think it is possible for ordinary people to choose to be extraordinary.”

普通人也可以选择不平凡。

“Persistence is very important. You should not give up unless you are forced to give up.”

坚持极其重要，除非被迫放弃，否则不要放弃。

“I would like to die on Mars. Just not on impact.”

我想死在火星上。但别是摔死的。

有出处的语录 · 已核实

与上方广为征引的格言不同，以下语录均经本站逐字核实、标注日期，并锚定到言行实录的对应条目——点击卡片即可跳转原文现场。

2002.10.03

“My proceeds from PayPal after tax were about $180M. $100M of that went into SpaceX, $70M into Tesla, and $10M into SolarCity. I had to borrow money for rent.”

我 PayPal 的税后所得大约 1.8 亿美元。1 亿投进 SpaceX，7000 万投进 Tesla，1000 万投进 SolarCity。然后我不得不借钱付房租。

言行实录 · 2012 访谈自述 → 2008.09.28

“As the saying goes, the fourth time's the charm… This is one of the best days of my life.”

常言道，第四次总会有好运……这是我人生中最好的日子之一。

Falcon 1 第四次发射 · Space.com → 2012.06.22

“It's about breaking a spell … It's showing that an electric car can be better than a gasoline car — it can be the best car, period.”

这关乎打破一个魔咒……它要证明电动车可以比燃油车更好——可以是最好的车，句号。

Model S 首批交付 · Forbes 现场报道 → 2013.08.12

“How could it be that the home of Silicon Valley and JPL – doing incredible things like indexing all the world’s knowledge and putting rovers on Mars – would build a bullet train that is both one of the most expensive per mile and one of the slowest?”

硅谷和 JPL 的老家——做着给全世界知识建索引、往火星放漫游车这种奇事的地方——怎么会修一条每英里最贵、速度又最慢的高铁之一？

Hyperloop Alpha 白皮书 → 2014.05.29

“It can land anywhere on Earth with the accuracy of a helicopter … I think that's what a spaceship should be able to do.”

它可以像直升机一样精准地降落在地球上的任何地方……我认为一艘飞船就应该能做到这一点。

Dragon V2 发布会 → 2014.06.12

“Tesla will not initiate patent lawsuits against anyone who, in good faith, wants to use our technology.”

Tesla 不会对任何善意使用我们技术的人发起专利诉讼。

开放专利博文 · Tesla 官方博客 → 2015.12.21

“This is a fundamental step change in technology compared to any other rocket that has ever flown.”

与史上飞过的任何其他火箭相比，这是技术上的根本性台阶变化。

猎鹰一级首陆 · 降落夜电信会议 → 2016.09.01

“Still working on the Falcon fireball investigation. Turning out to be the most difficult and complex failure we have ever had in 14 years.”

仍在跟进猎鹰火球的调查。结果证明这是我们 14 年来遇到过的最困难、最复杂的失败。

本人推文（2016.09.09）→ 2017.03.30

“This represents the culmination of 15 years of work at SpaceX to be able to refly a rocket booster. It’s really a great day, I think, for space as a whole.”

这代表着 SpaceX 十五年工作的集大成——让一枚火箭助推器可以再次飞行。我想，对整个航天事业来说，这真是了不起的一天。

SES-10 复飞发布会 → 2018.02.06

“I think it looks so ridiculous and impossible. And you can tell it’s real because it looks so fake, honestly. We’d have way better CGI if it was fake.”

我觉得它看起来太荒诞、太不可能了。你之所以能看出它是真的，恰恰是因为它看起来那么假——说实话，要真是假的，我们的特效早该做得好得多。

猎鹰重型首飞发布会 → 2018.08.07

“Am considering taking Tesla private at $420. Funding secured.”

正考虑以每股 420 美元将 Tesla 私有化。资金已到位。

本人推文 · SEC 诉讼与和解文件 → 2025.11.06

“There's not like an obvious limit. So like Optimus is kind of like an infinite money glitch. And maybe there won't even be money in the future.” — “Optimus will actually eliminate poverty… bigger than cell phones.”

这没有明显的上限。Optimus 有点像无限金钱外挂。也许未来甚至不会再有金钱这个东西。——Optimus 将真正消除贫困……比手机还要大（的市场）。
